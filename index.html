✨ The 4 Golden Rules of Laptop Ergonomics

🖥️

Eye-Level Screen

Raise your laptop on books or a stand so the top third of your display meets your eye line.

Prevents Tech-Neck
📐

The 90–90–90 Rule

Keep elbows, hips, and knees at 90° angles with both feet resting flat on the floor.

Balanced Spine
🦒

Tall Neck, Soft Shoulders
